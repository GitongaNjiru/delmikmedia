Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers regarding our production process, turnaround times, cinema equipment, and location shoots.

We shoot on professional cinema packages including RED, ARRI, and Sony FX series cinema cameras equipped with cinema primes, anamorphic lenses, wireless teradek transmitters, Ronin gimbal stabilizers, and professional boom/lavalier audio recorders (Sennheiser / Rode). For aerial cinematography, we deploy 4K/5.4K DJI drones.

Yes, absolutely! While our main headquarters is in Nairobi, our production crew travels throughout all 47 counties in Kenya (Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Maasai Mara, Eldoret) and across East Africa (Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda) for films, documentaries, and destination weddings.

For commercials (TVC) and social teasers, turnaround can be as fast as 24 to 72 hours. Corporate event highlight reels are typically delivered within 3 to 5 business days. Feature films, in-depth documentaries, and complex post-production projects vary based on scope and revision cycles.

We deploy a portable multi-camera broadcast switcher unit with professional audio integration, lower-thirds titling, and bonded cellular network routers (aggregating multiple SIM networks) ensuring uninterrupted 1080p/4K transmission to YouTube, Facebook, Zoom, or private corporate CDNs.

Booking is confirmed upon contract signing and a standard 50% deposit to secure the crew and shoot date. The remaining balance is payable upon delivery of approved final master edits. We accept Bank Transfers, M-Pesa, and international wire payments.
